# Ultimate Trading System: pictures and videos

Public copies of the cards and short videos made for
[Ultimate Trading System](https://ultimate-trading-system.com), so that posts and articles
can show them by address.

- `social/` the day's cards (square and upright) and short upright videos
- `brand/` the product's icon and banner
- `docs/` articles on testing trading rules for luck, in six languages ([start here](docs/README.md))

Software, not financial advice. Trading carries a substantial risk of loss.
